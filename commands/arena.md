---
description: Open the SkillMe Arena (answer quizzes in your browser while the AI keeps working)
---

Call the `skillme_status` tool with `open: true` to open the SkillMe Arena (in its own window when the app runtime is installed, otherwise in the browser).
Then reply in 2-3 short lines: the Arena link, the one-line scoreboard, and a reminder that quizzes now pop up there (with a sound, and a browser notification if they turn on the bell) so they never have to wait for you.
